### 🛡️ Mission Control

> **"Security is not a product, but a process of continuous reimagination."**

I am a **Information Security Professional** specializing in the intersection of **Threat Intelligence**, **Malware Research**, and **Artificial Intelligence**. My work revolves around bridging the gap between offensive research and defensive engineering. 

By day, I lead security operations and infrastructure hardening. By night, I dive into my lab to reverse-engineer malware, simulate APT lifecycles, and build AI agents that automate the hunt.
